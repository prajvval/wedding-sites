So what's a wedding microsite, really?

A small, beautiful website built specifically for your wedding. One link, shared on WhatsApp, that holds everything: your story, the schedule, the RSVP form, photos, venue map, travel info — all in one place. Your guests open it on their phone in two seconds.

01

Lives on WhatsApp

No envelopes, no stamps, no waiting for India Post. You forward one link to your family group and the entire invite is delivered.

02

Always up to date

Wedding got moved by an hour? Venue changed? You don't reprint 300 cards — we update the page and your guests see the latest version next time they tap.

03

RSVPs collect themselves

No chasing your chacha for confirmation. Every guest fills the form once, and you see a clean spreadsheet — who's coming, dietary needs, accommodation, plus-ones.

04

Everything in one place

Hotel options, airport pickups, dress codes for each event, the playlist for sangeet — your guests stop asking the same questions because the answers are right there.

05

Looks gorgeous on every phone

Designed mobile-first because that's how 95% of your guests will see it. Your friends in Australia and your Nani in Lucknow get the same beautiful experience.

06

Yours forever

The site stays live for months after your wedding. Add the official photos. Send guests home with a digital memory they can revisit anytime.

Standard & Premium · Included

Your wedding website doesn't end on your wedding day.

The day after your wedding, your microsite quietly transforms. The countdown disappears. The RSVP form steps aside. And in their place — a wedding photo album that lives at the same URL forever. We call it Eternal Memory Mode.

How it works

  • After the wedding, you email or WhatsApp us your favourite photos — phone shots, the photographer's edits, candid moments from family.
  • We sort them, optimise them for fast loading, and add them to your gallery. Your guests can revisit the site any time to look back.
  • Your story and event details stay on the page — guests who couldn't attend can still feel like they were there.

What you get

  • Standard — one batch update of up to 50 photos included, no extra charge. Hosting renewable at ₹1,500/year after the first year.
  • Premium — unlimited photo updates for the life of hosting. Add anniversary photos, baby announcements, family milestones — the site grows with your family.
  • One day, you can show your children the website where their parents got married.
