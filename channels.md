# Acquisition channels — Indian wedding microsite service

Channels are ranked by **confidence × ease of starting**. Every channel here
is reachable without organic Instagram posting.

---

## Tier 1 — start here

### 1. WedMeGood (vendor marketplace)

- **What it is:** India's largest wedding-vendor directory (~30k+ vendors,
  millions of couple pageviews/month). Couples actively shop the
  "Invitation Cards" / "Wedding Stationery" category.
- **Confidence:** Very high. This is the Zomato of wedding services.
- **Cost:** Free vendor listing tier exists. Paid "Featured" tier is
  ~₹15-30k/year and pushes you to the top of search for your city.
- **What you do:**
  1. Sign up at wedmegood.com → "Vendor Login" → "List your business".
  2. Category: "Invitations & Cards" → subcategory "Digital Invitations" /
     "E-Invites".
  3. Upload 8-12 portfolio images (your sample templates).
  4. Set city-level service area (start with your city, expand later).
  5. List package prices in your description (couples filter on price).
- **Realistic volume:** Free tier = 1-3 enquiries/month per active city.
  Paid tier = 8-20 enquiries/month. Conversion rate ~10-15%.

### 2. Wedding planners (Instagram cold-DM)

- **What it is:** Planners coordinate the whole wedding and get asked
  *"who do you recommend for invites?"* on every project.
- **Confidence:** Very high. This is industry-standard practice.
- **Cost:** Time only. Standard commission to planner: 10-15% of order value.
- **What you do:**
  1. Search Instagram: `wedding planner [your city]`,
     `[city] wedding planning`, `weddings of [city]`.
  2. Build a list of 30-50 active planners (recent posts, not dormant).
     Tier 2/3 cities = less competition, higher response rates than
     Mumbai/Delhi.
  3. DM each one (template in `outreach-templates.md`). Lead with
     their portfolio, not yours. Offer 15% commission, not 10% — beats
     market rate, gets you to top of mind.
  4. Follow up after 5 days if no reply. Standard.
- **Realistic volume:** Out of 50 DMs, ~10 reply, ~3-5 become recurring
  partners. Each recurring planner = 1-3 referrals/month in season.

### 3. Wedding photographers (Instagram cold-DM)

- **What it is:** Photographers book 6-12 weddings/month, meet the couple
  3-6 months before the wedding date — well before invites are sent.
- **Confidence:** Very high.
- **Cost:** Time + 10-15% commission.
- **What you do:** Same playbook as planners. Search hashtags:
  `#[city]weddingphotographer`, `#indianweddingphotography`.
  Filter for active accounts (posted in last 30 days).
- **Bonus angle:** Offer to feature their photos in your sample templates
  (with credit). Free portfolio piece for them, free imagery for you.

### 4. Banquet halls / 3-4 star hotels (walk-in)

- **What it is:** Venue managers maintain "preferred vendor" lists they
  hand to couples on enquiry.
- **Confidence:** High in Tier 2/3, medium in metros (more gatekeeping).
- **Cost:** Time + commission (varies, usually 10%).
- **What you do:**
  1. Make a list of every banquet hall + 3-4★ hotel within 30 km that
     hosts weddings. Google Maps → "wedding venues near me".
  2. Walk in midweek mornings (their slow time). Ask for the
     "events manager" or "wedding coordinator".
  3. Hand them a printed catalog (1-page A4 with QR code to your samples)
     and a business card. Offer 10% commission per closed referral.
  4. Follow up monthly with new sample images.
- **Realistic volume:** 1 in 5 venues will add you to their list. Each
  active venue = 1-2 referrals/month in season.

### 5. Bridal-wear & jewellery shops (walk-in + counter cards)

- **What it is:** Couples buy bridal lehenga / sherwani / jewellery 2-4
  months before the wedding — perfect timing for invite enquiries.
- **Confidence:** Medium-high. Better at independent local shops than
  chain stores (Tanishq HQ blocks vendor partnerships; local Tanishq
  franchisees may say yes informally).
- **What you do:**
  1. Visit local bridal stores. Ask to leave a small leaflet stand at
     billing counter.
  2. Offer staff a flat ₹500-1000 referral fee per closed wedding (no
     commission % — keeps it simple for them).
  3. Best targets: bridal lehenga rental shops, mid-tier jewellers,
     custom tailoring shops doing sherwanis.
- **Realistic volume:** 1-3 referrals/month per active partner.

## Tier 2 — add after Tier 1 is producing

### 6. Mehendi artists, makeup artists, decorators

- Small businesses, mostly solo operators, easy to DM. Booked 1-3 months
  pre-wedding. Same playbook as photographers/planners but smaller scale.
- Realistic volume: low per-partner (1 referral/2 months) but easy to
  recruit 10+ at once.

### 7. ShaadiSaga, WeddingSutra, BookEventz, WeddingWire India

- Secondary marketplaces. List for free; paid promotion has worse ROI
  than WedMeGood. Worth listing as a free traffic source.

### 8. Built-in virality (the long game)

- Every wedding microsite has a footer: *"made by [your brand]"* with a
  link to your service page.
- Every wedding has 200-500 guests. Of those, ~5-10% are themselves
  engaged or about to be.
- Realistic volume: zero in month 1. By month 6, this becomes 20-30%
  of inbound enquiries with no marketing cost.

## Tier 3 — niche / Tier 2-3 city only

### 9. Pandits / astrologers

- They consult couples for muhurat (auspicious wedding date) months
  before vendors get involved. Tier 2/3 city pandits often welcome
  ₹500-1000 per referral.
- Realistic volume: low and unreliable, but very early in the funnel.

### 10. Kalyana mandapams / community halls (South India specific)

- Same playbook as banquet halls but specifically for South Indian
  weddings. Often family-run, decisions are fast.

---

## What NOT to do

- **Buying lead lists / scraped phone numbers.** Illegal under DPDP Act
  2023, and the conversion is terrible because the leads are cold and
  ungated.
- **Government wedding registration offices.** Privacy issues, and the
  data is post-wedding (too late).
- **Paid Instagram/Facebook ads from day 1.** CPL for wedding services
  in India is ₹300-1500. Don't burn cash on ads until you have a proven
  conversion funnel and the unit economics to support it.
- **Cold-calling random "engaged" people from Shaadi.com profiles.** Same
  privacy + creepiness issue.

## First-week action plan

| Day | Task |
|---|---|
| Mon | Build prospect list: 50 planners + 30 photographers in your city |
| Tue | Create WedMeGood vendor profile (free tier) |
| Wed | DM 25 planners + 15 photographers (use templates) |
| Thu | DM remaining 25 + 15. Walk in to 3 nearby banquet halls |
| Fri | Follow up Monday DMs that didn't reply. Walk in to 3 jewellers |
| Sat | Reply to all incoming. Schedule calls. |
| Sun | Off (weddings are Sat-Sun, partners busy) |

Repeat next week with a fresh batch. Expect 2-4 first conversations from
week 1 outreach.
