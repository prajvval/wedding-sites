Honest answers

Common questions

Can I use this instead of printed cards entirely?

Most of our couples do exactly that — they save the printing cost (often ₹15-40k for 200 cards) and put it elsewhere. A small number do both: a single luxe printed card for grandparents and the immediate family, microsite for everyone else. Either way works.

What if my guests aren't tech-savvy?

If they can open a WhatsApp message, they can open the microsite. There's no app, no login, no signup. They tap the link, the site opens, they tap RSVP, they fill three fields. We've designed for grandparents.

Can the site be in Hindi / Tamil / Marathi?

Yes. Standard package includes English + 1 regional language with a toggle. Premium goes up to 3. Currently supported: Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi, Kannada, Malayalam, Bengali, Punjabi, Gujarati. Ask if you need another.

What if I want changes after delivery?

Each package includes edit rounds (1 / 2 / 5 for Basic / Standard / Premium). Beyond that, additional rounds are ₹500 each. We hold this line firmly — without it, hourly rates collapse and we can't keep prices low.

How do refunds work?

50% upfront, 50% on delivery. The 50% upfront is non-refundable once design starts (because we've already done the work). If you cancel before we begin design, full refund. Once your site is live and you've paid the balance, no further refunds — but we keep working on edit rounds until you're happy within your package limit.

Will my photos and guest data be safe?

Site is SSL-secured (green padlock). Guest RSVP data is stored in a private spreadsheet only you and we can access. Your photos are hosted on industry-standard image CDN — same providers used by major news sites. We never reuse your photos for marketing without your written permission.

What happens after the included hosting period?

Basic stays live for 6 months. Standard for 1 year, Premium for 2 years. After that, hosting is renewable at ₹1,500/year — most couples renew at least once because the site has become their wedding album. If you don't renew, the site is archived and we keep your final files for 2 more years in case you change your mind.

Can my wedding planner / photographer refer me?

Yes — and they'll get a 15% commission. Mention them in the enquiry form so we know to credit them. (Doesn't change your price.)
