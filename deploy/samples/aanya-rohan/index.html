In case you were wondering

FAQ

Can I bring a plus-one?

Our invitations are addressed individually because the Lake Palace is a small venue. If you'd like to bring a partner who isn't named on your invite, please drop us a note in the RSVP form and we'll do our best to accommodate.

Are children welcome?

Absolutely — please indicate the number of children in your RSVP so we can plan kid-friendly menu options and a small play area for the Sangeet evening.

What's the dress code?

Mehendi: sunlit yellows, pinks, mint. Sangeet: festive Indian formal. Wedding: traditional Indian formal. Reception: black tie or Indo-western. We'd love to see you in your finest, but please prioritise comfort — there's a lot of dancing planned.

Is the venue accessible?

The Lake Palace is reached by boat. The boats are stable and have step-on access; staff will assist anyone who needs help boarding. The palace itself has stairs but most ceremony areas are reachable via lift. If you have specific accessibility needs, let us know in the RSVP and we'll arrange the right path.

Gifts?

Your blessings and presence mean everything. If you'd still like to give something, we're collecting contributions towards a honeymoon in Kyoto — details will be shared with confirmed RSVPs closer to the date.

How can I reach the couple?

Aanya: +91 98765 43210 · Rohan: +91 98765 43211. We may not always pick up between events, but WhatsApp will reach us fastest.
