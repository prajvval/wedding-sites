# Outreach templates — copy-paste for partner DMs and walk-ins

All templates assume **English-first with Hindi fallback** (most Indian
wedding-industry pros work in English-Hinglish on professional channels).
Swap to regional language where the partner's Instagram bio suggests it.

Replace `{{...}}` placeholders before sending.

---

## 1. Wedding planner — Instagram cold DM

**Subject (if Instagram doesn't have subjects, use as opening line):**
> Quick partnership idea — wedding microsites for your couples

**Body:**
> Hi {{first_name}},
>
> Loved your work on the {{couple_name}} wedding — the {{specific_detail,
> e.g. "outdoor mandap setup", "haldi colour palette"}} was beautiful.
>
> I run a small studio that builds wedding microsites for couples — the
> kind of digital invite that gets shared on WhatsApp instead of (or
> alongside) a printed card. Each site has the couple's story, full
> event schedule, RSVP, gallery, venue maps, travel info. Packages
> start at ₹3,000.
>
> Would you be open to referring couples who'd like one? Standard 15%
> commission per closed referral, paid to you the same day the couple pays.
>
> Happy to send a few sample sites if helpful.
>
> {{your_name}}
> {{your_portfolio_url}}

**Why this works:** specific compliment first (not generic "love your
work"), one clear ask, commission rate stated upfront (15% beats the 10%
most studios offer), low-friction sample-on-request.

## 2. Wedding photographer — Instagram cold DM

**Body:**
> Hi {{first_name}},
>
> The {{couple_name}} shoot you posted last week — the {{detail,
> e.g. "candid mehendi frames", "low-light reception lighting"}} caught
> my eye.
>
> Quick idea: I build wedding microsites for couples (digital invite +
> RSVP + gallery + travel info). Most of your couples are 3-6 months
> out from their wedding when you book them — exactly when they're
> deciding on invites.
>
> If you ever want to refer a couple, I'd happily do 15% commission on
> closed work. I'd also love to feature 2-3 of your photos in my sample
> templates with full credit + a link back to your IG — free portfolio
> placement for you, beautiful imagery for me.
>
> Either way, your work is excellent. Happy weddings 🪔
>
> {{your_name}}

**Why this works:** offers value first (free portfolio placement) before
asking for the commission referral. Photographers are notoriously
oversaturated on commission-only pitches.

## 3. Banquet hall / hotel events manager — walk-in script

**Setup:** midweek morning (Tue-Wed-Thu, 10-11 am — slowest time at
wedding venues). Wear business casual, not formals. Carry: 5x A4 colour
prints of your sample sites + 20 business cards + a printed 1-page
catalog with QR code to your portfolio.

**Opening:**
> Hi, I'm {{name}}, I run a wedding microsite studio. Could I have 2
> minutes with whoever handles vendor partnerships?

If they ask "what is a wedding microsite":
> A small website for the wedding — invite, schedule, RSVP, gallery,
> Google Maps to your venue. Couples share the link on WhatsApp. Most
> couples in {{city}} are doing both digital + printed cards now.

**The pitch (when you have the events manager):**
> {{venue_name}} hosts maybe {{20-40}} weddings a year? I'd like to be
> on your preferred-vendor list for digital invitations. We have three
> packages starting at ₹3,000.
>
> Two things make it easy for you:
>
> 1. **Every site embeds your venue's Google Map and a "Getting here"
>    page.** Free advertising for {{venue_name}} in front of every guest.
> 2. **10% commission per closed referral**, paid the day the couple pays.
>
> Could I leave a one-page catalog with QR code to my samples? Happy to
> drop new portfolio images every month.

**Close:**
> What's the best email to send the catalog as a PDF?

(Always get the email — walk-ins get forgotten, emailed PDFs get filed.)

## 4. Bridal-wear / jewellery shop — counter pitch

**Setup:** walk in late afternoon (3-5 pm — between rush hours).

**Opening (to owner or manager):**
> Hi, I run a wedding microsite studio. Most of your customers are
> getting married in 2-4 months and still need to send out invites.
> Could I leave a small leaflet stand at your billing counter?

**The hook:**
> ₹1,000 per closed referral, paid in cash to whoever recommended us.
> No commission percentages, no follow-up paperwork — flat ₹1,000 the
> day we deliver the site to the couple.

**Why flat fee:** counter staff don't care about percentages; ₹1,000 cash
is concrete and motivating.

## 5. Couple enquiry — first reply (after they message you)

**Within 1 hour of their enquiry:**
> Hi {{name}}! Congratulations 🎉
>
> Thanks for reaching out. To send you the right package, just three
> quick questions:
>
> 1. **Wedding date** (or rough month if not finalised)?
> 2. **How many events** are you doing? (e.g. just reception, or
>    Mehendi + Sangeet + Wedding + Reception)?
> 3. **Roughly how many guests** will receive the invite?
>
> Once I know these, I'll send you 2-3 sample sites that match your style
> and a price quote within an hour.
>
> Looking forward to it!
>
> {{your_name}}

**Why this works:** congratulations first (Indian families pick up on
warmth), three specific questions (not "tell me about your wedding"
which gets long unfocused replies), commitment to fast turnaround.

## 6. Couple — quote + close

**After they reply with the 3 answers:**
> Hi {{name}},
>
> Based on your details ({{X events, Y guests, date}}), I'd recommend
> our **{{Standard / Premium}}** package — ₹{{price}}.
>
> What you get:
> {{bulleted list of inclusions}}
>
> **One thing most couples don't realise we include:** after your wedding
> day, your microsite quietly transforms into a wedding photo album. You
> send us your photos when you're ready — we add them to the gallery, and
> the same URL becomes your permanent wedding keepsake. We call it
> *Eternal Memory Mode*. {{Standard: 1 batch update included / Premium:
> unlimited updates included}}.
>
> **Sample sites in your style:** {{link 1}}, {{link 2}}
>
> **Delivery:** {{N days}} after we receive your photos and event details
>
> **Payment:** 50% to confirm (₹{{half}}), 50% on delivery. UPI / bank
> transfer / Razorpay link.
>
> Want to lock in the slot? I can send the payment link now.
>
> {{your_name}}

**Why this works:** package recommendation (not menu — reduces decision
fatigue), Eternal Memory Mode mention introduces emotional differentiator
right before the close (most studios pitch only the wedding-day deliverable),
payment terms upfront (no surprises), explicit ask to close.

## 7. Partner reactivation — monthly check-in

Send to inactive planners/photographers once per month:
> Hi {{name}}, hope wedding season is treating you well!
>
> Quick update — added 2 new templates this month: {{link}} and {{link}}.
> If any of your couples are deciding on invites, would love a referral.
>
> Also — if there's a style you keep getting asked for that I don't have
> yet, tell me and I'll build it next.
>
> {{your_name}}

**Why this works:** stays top of mind without being pushy, asks for
product feedback (signals you're investing in the partnership).

---

## What to track

For every partner DM/walk-in, log in a simple Google Sheet:

| Date | Channel | Partner name | City | First contact | Replied? | Referrals sent | Closed | Commission paid |

Without tracking, you can't tell which channels actually work. Aim for
20 active partners (replied + at least 1 referral) by month 3.
